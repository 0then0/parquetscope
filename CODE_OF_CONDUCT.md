# Code of conduct

ParquetScope welcomes contributors and users with different backgrounds and levels of experience. Help keep issues, pull requests, discussions, and other project spaces respectful and useful.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss ideas and evidence; give specific, constructive feedback.
- Respect different experiences, identities, and viewpoints.
- Accept corrections and take responsibility for the effects of your words and actions.
- Respect privacy. Share another person's personal information only with their permission.

Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, sexualized attention, personal attacks, and deliberate disruption are not acceptable. These expectations also apply when someone represents the project in another public space.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report concerns to the [project maintainer, 0then0](https://github.com/0then0). To request a private contact channel, use the [private contact request form](https://github.com/0then0/parquetscope/issues/new?template=private_contact.yml), select **Code of conduct**, and include no incident details or personal information. Private reports must use a channel agreed with the maintainer; do not post sensitive evidence in a public issue.

The maintainer may edit or remove harmful content, close or lock a discussion, issue a warning, or restrict participation, depending on severity and repeated behavior. Reports involving the maintainer cannot receive independent review through this project; GitHub's reporting and blocking tools remain available.

Participants should follow moderation decisions and avoid retaliation against people who raise concerns in good faith.
